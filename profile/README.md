# Microsoft Student Club – MU

> **Microsoft Student Club – MU (MCC MU)** is a student-led community at Mansoura University, affiliated with Microsoft through the **Microsoft Learn Student Ambassadors (MLSA)** program.

The club is officially recognized and approved by the **Faculty of Computers and Information, Mansoura University**, as part of the university’s student activities system.

MCC MU brings students together to **learn, collaborate, share knowledge, build practical experience, and create meaningful technical and non-technical initiatives**.

---

## 🏛️ Organization Structure

MCC MU operates through two complementary sides:

- **Technical Tracks** — responsible for technical learning, knowledge development, and hands-on experiences.
- **Operations Teams** — responsible for the organizational, communication, media, and logistical aspects that enable the club to operate effectively.

Together, these sides form the organizational structure that supports the club's **learning activities, projects, events, competitions, and community initiatives**.

---

# 💻 Technical Tracks
 
The Technical Department is organized into specialized tracks (Circles), each covering a key area of Computer Science and emerging technology. Every track follows a structured roadmap that defines its learning journey, topics, practical activities, and expected outcomes throughout the season.
 
## 🧭 Our Tracks
 
- **Cloud & DevOps**
- **Data Engineering**
- **Data Science**
- **Frontend**
- **Backend**
- **UI/UX**
## 📚 Learning Roadmaps
 
Each track maintains its own roadmap, designed to give members a clear, progressive path from fundamentals to real-world application.
 
A roadmap may include:
 
- Core concepts and fundamentals
- Advanced technical topics
- Hands-on exercises
- Practical projects
- Workshops and technical sessions
- Assessments and challenges
- Recommended learning resources
## 🔁 How Learning Works
 
Members learn through a simple weekly cycle:
 
1. **Kickoff** — the Mentor introduces the week's topic and shares the task and learning resources.
2. **Learn & Practice** — members study the material and complete the task on their own, asking for help early when blocked.
3. **Submit** — members deliver their work by the deadline.
4. **Review & Feedback** — the Mentor reviews each submission and gives clear feedback.
5. **Support Session (if needed)** — a session is held only when the review shows a concept needs extra explanation.
The goal is to help members grow from guided learning into confident, independent contributors.
 
## 👨‍🏫 Mentorship & Support
 
Each track is led by a Circle Head and supported by Mentors who guide members throughout their journey. Mentors are responsible for:
 
- Explaining and reinforcing technical concepts
- Supporting members during practical activities
- Providing regular guidance and feedback
- Helping members overcome technical challenges
- Tracking learning progress
- Supporting project development
## 🛠️ Technical Activities
 
Learning is delivered through a variety of activities, including:
 
- Workshops
- Technical sessions
- Tech talks
- Hands-on labs
- Projects
- Challenges
- Competitions
- Collaborative learning activities
## 🎯 Our Goal
 
Our goal is not only to teach technologies, but to give students the chance to apply what they learn, build real projects, collaborate with others, and gain practical, real-world experience.

# 🚀 Conbassador Program

The **Conbassador Program** is an internal initiative designed to support students who are interested in becoming **Microsoft Learn Student Ambassadors (MLSA)**.

The program helps interested students better understand the MLSA program, prepare for the application process, and work toward the requirements and activities expected from participants.

### 🎯 What We Provide

Through the Conbassador Program, students can receive:

- Guidance on the MLSA program
- Application preparation support
- Knowledge sharing from experienced members
- Follow-up throughout the application journey
- Support in understanding program requirements
- Guidance toward relevant learning and community activities

The program aims to help students build a stronger understanding of the MLSA journey and provide them with the guidance and resources needed to **prepare, apply, and grow within the program**.
